Quick verdict

Choose for your goal, not for hype.

Tinder offers broad reach, Hinge, Match, and eHarmony lean more relationship-first, Feeld specializes in openly negotiated nontraditional dating, and free-first options let you test local demand before spending.

No app guarantees matches, sex, dates, or a relationship. Profile quality, local activity, honest intent, timing, and mutual interest still decide the result.

Broad reach and faster discovery

Tinder, Bumble, and Plenty of Fish are the clearest volume-first starting points.

Relationship-first dating

Hinge, Match, eHarmony, and Coffee Meets Bagel put more weight on intention and filtering.

Alternative or explicit structures

Feeld is the specialist choice; OkCupid offers more room for values and relationship-structure detail.
