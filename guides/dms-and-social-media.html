DMs and social media openers

No matching algorithm has told her you're a fit. A cold DM has to earn its own credibility, and most men skip the part that builds it.

Audience: adults 18+Read time: 6 minutesOutcome: DMs that get read as genuine, not spam

Why this isn't the same as a dating app

She didn't opt into being approached here. Act like it.

On a dating app, a match means she's already open to hearing from you. On Instagram or TikTok, she posted for her own reasons, not to meet someone. That doesn't make DMing off-limits, but it means the burden of context is on you. A message that ignores that gets read as spam, because functionally, that's what it is without it.

Warm up before you ever send a DM

If you follow someone and immediately DM her, you're a stranger with a request. If you've genuinely engaged with a few posts first, a DM. Give it a few days and a couple of authentic interactions, not a like-spree on her whole profile in one sitting, which reads as exactly what it is.

The story reply is the best cold-open you have

Story reply

"That trail looks brutal. Which one is it?"

Reacts to something happening right now. Feels natural because it is.

Comment-to-DM

Leave a genuine comment publicly, then DM only if she replies

Lets her opt into a private conversation instead of you starting there uninvited.

Cold DM, no prior interaction

Use sparingly, and reference something specific from her page

Works occasionally. Works far less than a warmed-up approach.

What to skip

"Hey" or a compliment on her looks alone

Gives her nothing to respond to and reads as one of dozens she's already ignored.

Your own profile matters here too

She'll check your page before she replies. Make it worth the click.

A locked account, an empty grid, or a page that's obviously built to look impressive rather than lived-in all cost you credibility. You don't need a curated feed, just evidence of a real, active life.

What kills a social DM

  • Sexual or looks-only comments before any real exchange.
  • Mass-liking someone's entire feed in one sitting.
  • Sending a second message after one goes unanswered.
  • Long, unsolicited voice notes or paragraph DMs to someone you've never spoken to.
  • Treating a follow-back or story view as an invitation to escalate.

Next step

A good DM thread earns its way to a real conversation.

Once you're exchanging real messages, treat it like any other new connection: keep the momentum, then move it forward.
